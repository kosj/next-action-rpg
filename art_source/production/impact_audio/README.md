# Original layered impact audio

The nine WAVs in `game/assets/audio/impact/` are newly authored oscillator,
filtered-noise and inharmonic-resonator DSP, generated by
`tools/generate_impact_audio.py`. No recording, stock sample, model output,
account or paid service is used. The repository owner has not selected a
redistribution license for this original project audio (`not-selected`),
consistent with the project's existing code and original-art policy.

Reproduce with `python tools/generate_impact_audio.py`. The manifest pins the
generator hash, seeds 71031–71039, sample counts, SHA-256, PCM peak/RMS/DC and
actual file bytes. Format is 44.1kHz stereo 16-bit PCM, total 505,780 bytes.
Individual peak is 0.58; profiles use multiple lower-gain layers with 1.5dB group
headroom plus runtime live-voice attenuation. No delayed playback timers are
used. Audio's existing stop/WeakRef/epoch and Session master-volume policies
remain in charge.

The profile components are separate: short contact tick, light/heavy metal,
body thump, charged low end, debris tail, blocked metal, and two attack-start
air swings. Light contact uses 2 layers; heavy/skill 4; charged 5; blocked 3 with no
body/sub layer. `Audio.play_attack(attack_id,pitch)` is swing-only and runs even
for whiffs; `Audio.play_impact(context)` belongs to the confirmed-hit path.
Existing `play_sound` remains compatible. Concurrent effect players are capped
at 32, independently of the looped ambience. Valid confirmed sources are
`hurtbox_ray` and `hurtbox_proxy`; attack-start events cannot invoke a profile.

Heavy and charged swings start playback with the attack at pitch 1. The heavy
WAV contains 0.20s of quiet lead plus a 0.25s whoosh (envelope peak 0.325s,
end 0.45s). Charged contains 0.28s of quiet lead plus a 0.31s whoosh (envelope
peak 0.435s, end 0.59s). Actual PCM onset and waveform peaks are separately
recorded in the manifest. This baked timing adds no timer or async callback.
Audio follows its mixer clock while presentation hitpause may freeze the pose;
`stop_all` still cancels playback. Light-chain pitch variations remain separate.

Offline audition: `build/art-review/impact-audio/audition.wav`, ordered
light→heavy→charged→blocked with 350ms gaps. Exact timeline and measured peaks
are in `manifest.json`; current zero-density mix peaks are 0.096/0.169/0.208/
0.123 respectively. These are DSP/file checks, not a listening, loudness,
actual Web mix or AAA sound-design approval. Listen with the real scene and
confirm hit/whiff/block differentiation, latency, volume settings and crowd
headroom before final acceptance.
